# Source provenance

The static dense carrier and atlas generator (`cobweb.hpp`, `geometry.cpp`, `fibers.cpp`, `bake.cpp`, `export.cpp`) are preserved from the user's existing dense cobweb laboratory baseline. The impact module and benchmark harness are a separate later prototype. No external geometry, downloaded textures, image-generation output or bundled third-party libraries are included. Existing repository licensing continues to apply; this prototype adds no new licensing terms.
