# Shared geometric damage on procedural assembly parts

Status: **verified integration slice; global damage goal remains ACTIVE; Beauty PARTIAL**.

The same selected `DamageArea` now applies Cavity, Dent, or Through Hole to any closed assembly Part. The existing selected `DamagePathCutter` applies Crack to those parts. Selection uses the semantic Part ID, role, index or path interval, and protection prevents the cut. The operations preserve the source recipe; moving the area away restores the original mesh. The cutter adds triangles around the affected region, keeps the other Parts' stable IDs, and marks newly exposed faces with a fresh interior region. Dent depth is capped by local size and thickness; Through Hole extends through the selected solid. The selected surface is raycast so inclined bridge planks receive shallow damage at the intended point.

The v4 assembly damage recipe roundtrips Cavity, Dent and Through Hole; older schemas retain their previous interpretation. UV transfer uses a bounded spatial search instead of testing every source triangle for every output face.

Evidence: Release `ALL_BUILD` passed and CTest passed **25/25** (`../2026-09-27-universal-damage-build.log`, `../2026-09-27-universal-damage-ctest.log`). The focused fixture cuts one intact solid in each of the 14 assembly families with all four geometric choices, validates closed output, fresh region and stable Part ID, and distinguishes dent floor from a true through exit by raycast. The Vulkan capture on an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3090 produced 27 original 1280×960 PNGs; a second capture reproduced all 27 image hashes exactly. Of the 23 prior cases, only `pipe-cavity.png` changed because its projector was placed on the actual surface. Representative generation times from `procedural-assemblies-proof.json`: pipe baseline 1.11 ms, dent 24.70 ms, cavity 25.93 ms, through hole 25.61 ms; pier baseline 0.61 ms, plank dent 3.35 ms, cavity 3.39 ms, through hole 3.66 ms.

Visual review: `plank-dent.png` retains a floor, `plank-through-hole.png` shows an open exit, and `pipe-through-hole.png` shows a lateral opening. The cuts still look machined and monochrome. Edge Damage, material aging, physically activated variants and the rest of the cross-consumer matrix in the shared damage authority are not complete. These captures do not establish natural destruction or global acceptance.
