# Edge Damage on selected assembly parts

Status: **verified integration slice; global damage goal ACTIVE; Beauty PARTIAL**.

`AreaOperation::EdgeDamage` now uses the shared `SurfaceFeatureFields` and `sampleEdgeDamage` to select an eligible crease on a semantic Part in each of the 14 assembly families. The same local `DamageArea` cutter used for other geometric operations creates a chip and fresh interior, keeping the source recipe editable and movable. The UI exposes curvature policy, metric or relative width, curvature interval, endpoint taper, and strength. Width changes the cutter footprint; strength and taper change its depth. Assembly damage recipe v5 records these settings; v4 cannot silently reinterpret Edge Damage.

The previous shared field had per-vertex curvature and edge distance but no physical crease segment. A long plank side can have its closest vertex far outside a local projector even when the projector touches the edge. That concrete fixture required adding `featureEdges` to the **shared** surface field, with endpoints, signed angle and outward normal; the assembly generator does not calculate a private curvature field. The cutter is bounded by the chosen segment, including its irregular rim and projector rotation. The focused tests cover a flat-center no-op, a plank chip, endpoint taper, width and strength response, linear combination of area/edge strength, closed irregular cuts and one selected intact solid in each assembly family.

Release `ALL_BUILD` and **25/25** CTest passed (`../2026-09-27-assembly-edge-build.log`, `../2026-09-27-assembly-edge-ctest.log`). Vulkan on NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3090 produced 29 original 1280×960 captures; a second run reproduced all 29 image hashes, and the 27 prior cases stayed byte-identical. See `procedural-assemblies-proof.json`, `plank-edge.png`, and `pipe-edge.png`. The plank edge chip is visible, but mechanically smooth. The pipe rim chip changes the mesh (4024 to 4358 triangles) yet is barely perceptible in the capture. This is technical integration evidence, **not** acceptance of natural destruction.

Remaining global work includes Infinite Fracture's semantic broken variants and physics, Missing Part and aging controls, shared damage across splines/geology/webs where applicable, interactive review of controls, and convincing material/Beauty results. This slice tests one representative Part per family, not every role and geometry variant.
