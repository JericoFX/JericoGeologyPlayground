# Cave macroform experiment — 2026-09-25

Status: **PASS for an opt-in BSP/vector-FBM high→low experiment; PARTIAL for natural-cave Beauty**. JericoEngine and Blender were not modified or run.

`--cave-geology-bakes-test` retains the original subtractive BSP arch/tunnel and applies the playground's deterministic native vector-FBM field after CSG to the arched mesh at each subdivision. Seed 123, amplitude 0.45, frequency 0.55, three octaves, lacunarity 1.87, coordinate scale `(0.65,0.7,0.8)` and output scale `(0.6,0.5,1)` displace the exterior and tunnel coherently in XYZ; the high alone then gets its existing finer normal-noise detail. The square tunnel is an unchanged control. The [copied M8 cave reference](../docs/engine-reference-2026-09-24/additional/docs/evidence/M8_SPARSE_SDF_CAVE_TUNNEL_SHOWCASE.md) instead describes tapered irregular rings, a spline tunnel, a chamber and a branch; this experiment does **not** implement those landmarks.

The focused RED initially failed at link time for the absent geological source, then failed at LOD2 because `evaluateSource` requires a nominal resolution of at least four even when deforming an already subdivided source. The final function passes `max(4, subdivisions)` without increasing its actual low mesh subdivision. `Playground.BspSource` now checks selected BSP identities, deterministic replay, physical exterior depth change, closed topology at subdivisions 2/4/6/8 and no locally inverted faces; the square remains byte-for-byte unchanged.

The original-resolution Vulkan front/oblique [visual audit](2026-09-25-cave-geology-visual.json) measured arch silhouette IoU 0.949/0.969 against a same-camera control, with 16,995/10,746 changed mask pixels. The [bake audit](2026-09-25-cave-geology-audit.json) passed all six 1024-square high→low packages: arch 5,376 high triangles to 3,024/1,344/336 low triangles, sampled surface error 0.101/0.101/0.113 below the 0.15 diagnostic ceiling, zero misses/backfaces, no unpainted UV interiors, matching specialist mip coverage and no fabricated region IDs. Square geometry/material captures and 131 compared PNG/OBJ artifacts remained identical to the prior control. Arch bake CPU times were 1,313/1,155/973 ms versus control 1,318/1,134/944 ms; these single runs are not a controlled performance acceptance. The Release build and full CTest 17/17 passed.

Visual inspection: the warped arch is visibly less planar, but the oblique view still reads as a prismatic block with a hole. The interior lacks a chamber, curved branch, geological stratification and interior lighting. Only seed 123 was captured. `PA::validateClosedMesh` plus per-face orientation do not prove absence of all global self-intersections. Keep this variant opt-in; next geometry work should build a true tapered/ringed entrance and deeper connected negative space rather than add more surface noise to the slab. The CC0 diffuse source and SHA-256 are in `Evidence/cc0-references/manifest.json`.
